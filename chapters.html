Start a Chapter

Starting a Code Connect chapter is a great way to bring coding and technology education to your school or community. Our chapters are led by passionate individuals who want to make a difference.

As a chapter leader, you will receive support from our team, access to resources, and the opportunity to connect with other chapter leaders. We provide everything you need to get started, including curriculum materials, event planning guides, and promotional materials.
